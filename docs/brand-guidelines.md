# BondSBA product voice and visual identity

Owner direction, September 10, 2026: modern and high-tech, but trustworthy and free of generic AI styling or copy.

Use BondSBA consistently. Describe the product as cash planning and WIP review for contractors. Lead with the actual task: forecast receipts and payments, compare job totals, prepare a packet. Avoid slogans, invented scores, claims of guaranteed approval, and vague promises to transform a business.

Use graphite and navy surfaces, muted mint actions, legible financial figures and restrained borders. Reserve warm colors for losses and exceptions. Avoid neon glow, tilted financial panels, decorative grids, pulsing activity indicators, confetti and fake urgency. Interactions should confirm actual actions. Respect reduced motion and keep print outputs plain.

Label fictional examples prominently. Show calculation methods and limitations where users can find them. Describe local storage precisely: this workspace does not upload inputs; a saved copy belongs to the browser profile, and backups contain the entered data. Do not broaden that claim to unrelated product routes.

Keep the real contact link, privacy policy and terms accessible. Do not invent a company address, credentials, certifications, user counts, endorsements, customer logos, testimonials, security seals or affiliation with SBA. Add proof only after verifying it. Marketing research is not an endorsement.

Preserve existing saved data, input labels and financial logic when refining presentation. Check the layout on mobile and desktop, example labels, focused controls, exports and printed packets before release.
